PHASE 4: BRUHAT-TITS TREE INTERPRETATION
================================================================================

  On the Bruhat-Tits tree T_p (the p-adic analogue of the hyperbolic plane):
  - Boundary points = elements of P^1(Q_p) = Q_p and the point at infinity
  - Cross-ratio = projective invariant under PGL(2, Q_p) action on T_p
  - ord_p(CR) = signed graph distance between the geodesics determined by the 4 points
  
  A non-zero valuation indicates the 4 Compton frequencies are NOT in general
  position on the BT tree — they have p-adic geometric structure.
  
  A zero valuation (CR is a p-adic unit) means the 4 points are in general
  position — no special p-adic geometric configuration.
  
  If |ord_p(CR)| ≥ 1 for some prime p, then:
  - The particle frequencies occupy a SPECIAL geometric configuration on T_p
  - This is a genuine, pre-registered test of the adelic hypothesis
  - The NULL hypothesis (no structure) predicts uniform random valuations
