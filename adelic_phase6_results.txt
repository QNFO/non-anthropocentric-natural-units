PHASE 6.5: SYSTEMATIC UNCERTAINTY ANALYSIS
================================================================================

  Key systematic uncertainties:
  
  1. Motive ambiguity: Class number 1 gives 9 discriminants, but only 3 particle
     generations. Alternative motives (class number 2, 3) could shift predictions
     by factors of O(10^3-10^6).
     
  2. j-invariant mapping: The exact mapping from CM j-invariants to particle masses
     is not uniquely determined. We test cube-roots, direct ratios, and log-matching.
     Alternative mappings could change mass predictions by ~20-50%.
     
  3. L-function truncation: The Monster L-function is a complex analytic object.
     Our Euler-product approximation uses only the 15 supersingular primes.
     Full L-function computation could shift Lambda estimate by orders of magnitude.
     
  4. CMB template model dependence: The log-periodic template assumes uniform
     amplitudes A_p. A more sophisticated model with p-dependent amplitudes
     could change detection prospects significantly.
     
  5. g-2 hadronic uncertainties: The muon g-2 SM prediction has significant
     hadronic vacuum polarization uncertainties. The prime-sum hypothesis
     assumes the anomaly is purely adelic — if part is hadronic, C_qed shifts.
