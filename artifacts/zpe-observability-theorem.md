## §1. Research Question

Pillar IV of the RG-Harmonic Isomorphism asserts: "Zero-point energy is the fixed-point vacuum expectation — unobservable without gravity." This claim is widely accepted among practitioners but has never, to our knowledge, been formalized as a rigorous theorem with precise scope conditions, explicit assumptions, and a systematic edge-case analysis.

**The specific question:** Can we state and prove a no-go theorem: "In any Poincaré-invariant QFT with only local interactions and no dynamical gravity, the absolute zero-point energy density is unobservable — only energy differences between configurations produce measurable effects"?

The practical importance is high. Claims of "zero-point energy harvesting" appear periodically in the fringe-physics and patent literature. A precise theorem would provide the rigorous basis for dismissing such claims without re-litigating the underlying physics each time. The Casimir effect, often cited as evidence for observable ZPE, is a configurational energy difference — perfectly consistent with the theorem. The cosmological constant, the one place absolute ZPE *is* observable, requires gravity — also consistent with the theorem.

## §2. Literature Review

### 2.1 Key Precedents

**Jaffe (2005), "The Casimir Effect and the Quantum Vacuum," PRD 72, 021301(R).**
Jaffe argues that the Casimir force can be derived entirely from source-source interactions (van der Waals forces in the retarded regime) without reference to zero-point fluctuations of the vacuum. The Casimir energy between two plates is a difference between the electromagnetic mode structure in the presence and absence of conductors — it never requires assigning an absolute value to the vacuum energy. Jaffe's formulation is: "The Casimir effect gives no more (or less) support for the reality of the vacuum energy of fluctuating quantum fields than any other one-loop effect in quantum electrodynamics."

**Weinberg (1989), "The Cosmological Constant Problem," Rev. Mod. Phys. 61, 1.**
Weinberg's seminal review frames the CC problem as: why is the observed vacuum energy density ~10⁻⁴⁷ GeV⁴ when naive QFT expectations give ~10⁷⁶ GeV⁴? The crucial point [established] is: the absolute vacuum energy is only observable through its gravitational effects — the curvature of spacetime. No non-gravitational experiment can detect a uniform, spacetime-constant energy density. This is because only *gradients* of the energy-momentum tensor produce measurable non-gravitational effects, and a constant vacuum energy has zero gradient.

**Polchinski (2006), "The Cosmological Constant and the String Landscape," arXiv:hep-th/0603249.**
Polchinski notes that in string theory, the CC receives contributions from every field and every compactification, but the observable consequence is purely gravitational. The "landscape" anthropic argument is precisely that non-gravitational physics cannot detect which vacuum we inhabit.

### 2.2 The Gap

None of these works state the no-go theorem in a formal, self-contained way with explicit assumptions and a systematic classification of edge cases. Jaffe focuses on the Casimir effect specifically; Weinberg and Polchinski focus on the CC problem. What is missing is a unified theorem statement that covers all non-gravitational experiments.

## §3. Formal Theorem Statement

### 3.1 Definitions

Let a quantum field theory be defined by:
- A Hilbert space H carrying a unitary representation U(a, Λ) of the Poincaré group (translations a^μ, Lorentz transformations Λ)
- A unique Poincaré-invariant vacuum state |0⟩ satisfying U(a, Λ)|0⟩ = |0⟩
- A set of local quantum fields φ_i(x) satisfying microcausality: [φ_i(x), φ_j(y)] = 0 for spacelike-separated (x − y)² < 0
- A Hamiltonian H = ∫ d³x T^00(x) where T^μν(x) is the conserved, symmetric energy-momentum tensor

Define the **absolute vacuum energy density**:

$$ \rho_{\text{vac}} \equiv \langle 0 | T^{00}(x) | 0 \rangle $$

By translation invariance, this is independent of x.

Define an **observable** O as any Hermitian operator that can be expressed as a function of local fields at finite spacetime points, integrated over compact spacetime regions (i.e., any realistic detector's measurable). Formally, O is in the algebra generated by fields smeared with test functions of compact support.

### 3.2 Theorem (ZPE Observability No-Go)

**Theorem.** In any Poincaré-invariant quantum field theory satisfying:
1. **Poincaré invariance:** The vacuum |0⟩ is the unique translation-invariant state
2. **Locality:** All observables are constructed from local fields satisfying microcausality
3. **No dynamical gravity:** Spacetime is fixed Minkowski space; the metric is non-dynamical
4. **No topology change:** The spacetime manifold is simply connected ℝ^(1,3) with trivial topology

the absolute vacuum energy density ρ_vac is unobservable. That is, for any observable O constructed from local fields as defined above:

$$ \langle 0 | O | 0 \rangle \text{ is independent of } \rho_{\text{vac}} $$

Equivalently: shifting the Hamiltonian by a constant H → H + c·I for any real c changes no expectation value, transition amplitude, or correlation function of any local observable.

### 3.3 Proof Sketch

**Step 1: Vacuum energy shifts are total derivatives.**

In any local QFT, the energy-momentum tensor is defined only up to total derivative terms ("improvement terms"):
$$ T^{\mu\nu} \to T^{\mu\nu} + \partial_\rho \partial_\sigma X^{\mu\rho\nu\sigma} $$
where X has the symmetries of the Riemann tensor and vanishes when integrated. Adding a constant c to T^00 corresponds to the improvement X^0ρ0σ = c·η^{ρσ}/(4·3), which is a total derivative of a boundary term. [established: standard QFT result, see e.g. Freedman-van Proeyen §3.2]

**Step 2: Translation invariance forces factorization.**

The vacuum is an eigenstate of the momentum operator: P^μ|0⟩ = 0. The Hamiltonian H = P^0. For any observable O(x) = e^{iP·x} O(0) e^{−iP·x}, the vacuum expectation value is:
$$ \langle 0 | O(x) | 0 \rangle = \langle 0 | e^{iP·x} O(0) e^{−iP·x} | 0 \rangle = \langle 0 | O(0) | 0 \rangle $$
independent of x. Adding a c-number constant to H multiplies every state by e^{−ict} — a global phase that drops out of all expectation values and transition amplitudes.

**Step 3: Only differences are measurable.**

Any physical measurement compares two configurations: the system with and without an apparatus, two different boundary conditions, or the system at two different times. The absolute vacuum energy subtracts out in every such comparison because it contributes identically to both configurations. This is the core of the theorem:

For any two configurations A and B of local fields that differ only on compact spatial regions:
$$ \Delta E_{A \to B} \equiv \langle B | H | B \rangle − \langle A | H | A \rangle $$
has no dependence on ρ_vac because ρ_vac · (Volume) cancels exactly.

**Step 4: Gravitational coupling is the sole exception.**

The energy-momentum tensor couples to gravity via Einstein's equations:
$$ R_{\mu\nu} − \frac{1}{2}g_{\mu\nu}R = 8\pi G_N T_{\mu\nu} $$
A constant term in T_μν = ρ_vac · g_μν contributes to R but NOT to any divergence ∂_μ T^{μν} (which vanishes identically for a constant). Since non-gravitational forces couple to T^{μν} only through its divergence (or through local interactions that depend on field gradients), a constant vacuum energy cannot generate non-gravitational forces. Only gravity — which couples to T^{μν} directly, not its divergence — "sees" the absolute value.

## §4. Edge Case Analysis

### 4.1 The Casimir Effect

**Claim:** "The Casimir effect proves zero-point energy is real and observable."

**Analysis:** The Casimir force arises from the difference in electromagnetic mode structure between two configurations:
- Configuration A: two conducting plates at separation d
- Configuration B: no plates (or plates at infinite separation)

The Casimir energy E_C(d) = −π²ħc/(720 d³) per unit area is the *difference* between these configurations' vacuum energies. The absolute energy of either configuration diverges as Λ⁴ (UV cutoff); only the difference is finite, cutoff-independent, and physically meaningful.

**Verdict:** Consistent with the theorem. The Casimir effect measures a configurational energy difference, not an absolute ZPE. Jaffe (2005) showed the same force can be derived without referencing vacuum fluctuations at all.

### 4.2 The Dynamical Casimir Effect

**Claim:** "Moving mirrors create photons from the vacuum, proving ZPE is real."

**Analysis:** The dynamical Casimir effect — where accelerated boundaries create real photon pairs from the vacuum — also satisfies the theorem. The energy of the created photons comes from the mechanical work done on the moving boundary, not from extracting absolute vacuum energy. What changes is the boundary condition (a configurational difference), and the energy difference between the initial and final states is provided by the external agent moving the mirror.

**Verdict:** Consistent. The dynamical Casimir effect measures the difference in vacuum structure between stationary and accelerating boundary conditions. No absolute ZPE is extracted.

### 4.3 The Unruh Effect

**Claim:** "An accelerating observer sees a thermal bath, implying the vacuum has real energy content."

**Analysis:** The Unruh effect states that an observer with constant proper acceleration a detects a thermal bath at temperature T = ħa/(2πck_B). This is a detector response, not an extraction of energy from the vacuum. The detected particles come from the interaction Hamiltonian coupling the detector to the field — the energy is supplied by the external agent accelerating the detector.

Crucially, an inertial observer in the same region of spacetime detects the Minkowski vacuum — no particles, no temperature. The effect is observer-dependent. The absolute vacuum energy (as defined in the inertial frame) has not changed.

**Verdict:** Consistent with the theorem. The Unruh effect is a detector response to the vacuum's correlation structure, not an extraction of absolute ZPE.

### 4.4 The Schwinger Effect

**Claim:** "Strong electric fields create electron-positron pairs from the vacuum, extracting energy from it."

**Analysis:** The Schwinger effect — pair production in a strong background electric field with rate ∝ exp(−πm²/|eE|) — is a non-perturbative tunneling process. The energy for pair creation comes from the external electric field, which does work on the virtual pair, promoting it to a real pair. The vacuum state is modified by the background field (it becomes an unstable "false vacuum"), but the energy balance is: field energy lost = rest mass of created pair + kinetic energy.

**Verdict:** Consistent with the theorem. The Schwinger effect is a field-energy-to-particle-energy conversion, mediated by vacuum fluctuations, not an extraction of absolute ZPE.

### 4.5 Lamb Shift

**Claim:** "The Lamb shift is a vacuum fluctuation effect — doesn't that mean ZPE is observable?"

**Analysis:** The Lamb shift — the splitting of the 2S_{1/2} and 2P_{1/2} levels in hydrogen — is a one-loop QED radiative correction. In modern QFT, it is computed from the electron's self-energy and vertex corrections without any reference to absolute zero-point energy. The effect is the *difference* in radiative corrections between two energy levels. The absolute ZPE of the electromagnetic field cancels.

**Verdict:** Consistent. The Lamb shift measures a differential radiative correction, not an absolute ZPE.

### 4.6 Cosmological Constant (Gravity)

**Claim:** "The cosmological constant IS observable — doesn't that disprove the theorem?"

**Analysis:** The CC is observable precisely because gravity couples to T^{μν} directly, not its divergence. This is the explicit exception built into the theorem's assumption #3 ("no dynamical gravity"). The fact that the CC is the *only* way absolute ZPE is observable supports the theorem, not refutes it — the theorem correctly identifies gravity as the unique coupling that breaks the no-go result.

**Verdict:** The CC is the exception that proves the rule. It demonstrates that the theorem's scope conditions are correct: remove "no dynamical gravity" and the no-go result fails — but in precisely the way the theorem predicts.

## §5. Scope and Limitations

### 5.1 Explicit Assumptions

The theorem relies on four assumptions. Weakening any of them allows absolute ZPE observability:

| Assumption | If Violated | Example |
|------------|-------------|---------|
| **Poincaré invariance** | Spacetime-dependent vacuum energy (e.g., inhomogeneous cosmology) | Varying scalar field potentials |
| **Locality** | Non-local correlations may produce absolute energy signals | Hypothetical non-local hidden variable theories |
| **No dynamical gravity** | Absolute vacuum energy becomes observable through curvature | Cosmological constant, dark energy |
| **No topology change** | Topological contributions to vacuum energy (e.g., wormholes, compact dimensions) | Casimir energy in compactified extra dimensions |

### 5.2 What the Theorem Does NOT Say

- **Does NOT say** the vacuum is "empty" or "nothing" — vacuum fluctuations, virtual particles, and field correlations are real physical phenomena
- **Does NOT say** the Casimir effect, Lamb shift, or Unruh effect are unreal — they are real, measurable effects that involve the vacuum's correlation structure
- **Does NOT say** absolute ZPE doesn't exist — only that it's unobservable without gravity
- **Does NOT solve** the cosmological constant problem — it clarifies *why* the CC problem is special (gravity is the unique probe of absolute vacuum energy), not *what* the CC value should be

### 5.3 RG-Harmonic Interpretation

Pillar IV reframes this theorem in RG language: the zero-point energy is the *fixed-point vacuum expectation*. Under RG flow, the absolute value of the vacuum energy is cutoff-dependent (it scales as the fourth power of the UV cutoff) — it is not an RG-invariant observable. Only *differences* in vacuum energies between configurations (which are UV-finite and scheme-independent) have physical meaning.

The harmonic oscillator perspective: E_0 = (1/2)ħω is the ground-state eigenvalue of H. Adding a constant to H shifts all eigenvalues by the same amount, which is equivalent to redefining the zero of energy. This is a gauge choice, not a physical observable. The theorem formalizes this obvious fact of quantum mechanics at the level of QFT.
